## Discovery is not qualification

A business appearing in a search result makes it a candidate, not a qualified prospect. Qualification begins only after you confirm the company, its current website, and a current observation that relates to the service you sell.

## The evidence test

A useful finding should be factual, current, observable, relevant to your service, and meaningful to the business. A subjective preference about colors or typography is usually weak evidence. A broken estimate path on a current service page is concrete.

## Connect the finding to a consequence

The purpose is not to criticize a website. It is to understand whether the finding can affect a business outcome: a prospective customer cannot request a quote, key information is inaccessible on mobile, or a conversion path fails.

## Rejection is a valid result

If the evidence is weak, stale, or unrelated to your offer, reject the candidate. A disciplined system can produce zero qualified prospects from a batch. That is better than manufacturing a sales angle.
