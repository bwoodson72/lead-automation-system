question: When should I use Agency Partnership Playbook instead of Client Acquisition Without Upwork?
answer: >-
  Client Acquisition Without Upwork is the broader system for building and operating an independent pipeline across acquisition channels. Agency Partnership Playbook is specifically for making agencies a deliberate source of paid work: qualifying the relationship, testing it on paid engagements, protecting scope and margin, measuring the real cost to serve, and deciding how much capacity the relationship has earned. Use the Agency Partnership Playbook when agency relationships themselves are the channel you want to develop. Use Client Acquisition Without Upwork when the larger problem is creating and managing qualified opportunities across the business.
