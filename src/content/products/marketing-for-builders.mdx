reframe: |-
  Most independent developers do not need another list of marketing ideas. You already know you could improve SEO, publish articles, post on LinkedIn, ask for referrals, start a newsletter, join communities, redesign the website, or try another channel.

  The problem is that any of those activities can be reasonable and still be the wrong next move. That creates a familiar cycle. The pipeline gets quiet, so you change the website, add a channel, publish a few things, or chase search traffic. Client work picks up and the activity stops. Because several things changed at once, you learn very little about what actually helped. The next quiet period begins with the same question: what am I supposed to do now?

  Marketing for Builders gives you the decision system underneath the tactics. You decide which market deserves focused attention, what a qualified buyer needs to understand and believe, what evidence can support that argument, and where the right people can realistically encounter it. Then you operate the system within the time you actually have. When results disappoint, you diagnose whether the problem is market choice, the argument, proof, access, or execution before you spend more time changing things.

faqs:
  - question: Is this mainly a content-marketing guide?
    answer: No. Content comes after more basic decisions. The system first establishes who you are trying to become relevant to, what makes the work matter, what a buyer should understand and believe, and what evidence supports that argument. Content is one way to carry that argument, not the strategy itself.
  - question: Will this guarantee leads or clients?
    answer: No. The product does not control demand, timing, buyer decisions, or channel performance. It gives you a disciplined way to make and operate the marketing decisions that are under your control, collect useful evidence, and decide what deserves attention next.
  - question: How is this different from Client Acquisition Without Upwork?
    answer: Client Acquisition Without Upwork is about creating individual opportunities through targeting and direct acquisition activity. Marketing for Builders builds the market understanding, argument, proof, buyer-facing infrastructure, access routes, and management loop that make you easier to understand, evaluate, encounter, and remember over time.
  - question: Do I need to choose one permanent niche before I can use this?
    answer: No. The book teaches a market hypothesis, not a niche oath. You choose a direction specific enough to investigate, record why it is reasonable, and write down the evidence that would make you narrow, broaden, change, or drop it.
  - question: What if I do not have strong case studies or quantified client outcomes?
    answer: The system does not tell you to invent them. You learn to match claims to the evidence you actually have, including technical artifacts, demonstrations, process evidence, project records, verified delivered states, and client evidence where it legitimately exists.
  - question: Will I have to post every day or follow a fixed marketing calendar?
    answer: No. The operating section starts from your real delivery capacity and builds a rhythm that can survive client work. Routes, maintenance obligations, activation, and review are kept deliberately bounded so marketing does not become a second full-time job.
  - question: What does the workbook actually do?
    answer: The 20 modules turn the guide into an operating record. You build and update a Market Model, Market Argument, Market Access record, and Marketing Management record, then carry those decisions forward instead of starting from a blank page every time you change a message, asset, channel, or experiment.
