question: How do orders, downloads, refunds, and product support work?
answer: Lemon Squeezy handles checkout, order history, receipts, and access to the digital files attached to your purchase. Use Lemon Squeezy My Orders when you need to recover an order or download. Developer Business Lab handles questions about using the products, problems with product materials, affiliate questions, website issues, and refund requests under the posted DBL refund policy. Submit refund requests through the Developer Business Lab Support Center and choose Refund request as the support category. The Support Center routes order administration to Lemon Squeezy and DBL-specific issues to the appropriate support path.
