<ProductSection eyebrow="The system" title="Seven decisions turn marketing into an operating loop." tone="muted">

1. **Market** — choose one specific, reachable buyer.
2. **Problem** — identify a painful problem with a meaningful consequence.
3. **Offer** — connect your technical capability to an outcome the buyer values.
4. **Message** — explain the relevance, problem, consequence, outcome, proof, and next step.
5. **Channel** — choose one practical way to reach the buyer instead of spreading effort everywhere.
6. **Action** — source and contact real prospects or users through the First 25 Challenge.
7. **Measurement** — read the results like an engineer and improve the weakest stage.

The purpose is not to make you a full-time marketer. It is to give you a small system that produces evidence instead of guesswork.

</ProductSection>

<ProductSection eyebrow="What you will actually do" title="The guide moves from decisions to real market contact.">

You will define your buyer, build a problem hypothesis from evidence, turn that problem into an offer, write a buyer-first message, and choose a primary acquisition channel. Then you will build your first prospect or user list and put the system into motion.

The **First 25 Challenge** is deliberately small. Twenty-five prospects is not presented as a conversion benchmark. It is a learning sprint designed to give you enough real-world feedback to see where the acquisition loop is breaking down.

You will also get qualification guidance, practical outreach templates, a seven-day implementation plan, a one-page desk reference, and a complete worked example that follows the system from market selection through results.

</ProductSection>

<ProductSection eyebrow="Who it is for" title="A starting system for builders who need customers, not more theory." tone="muted">

This is designed for freelance developers finding clients, technical consultants shaping commercial offers, SaaS founders validating demand, and independent builders who understand the technical work but have never built a customer-acquisition process.

It is **not** a promise of instant revenue, guaranteed customers, bulk-spam tactics, or passive acquisition. The goal is a working feedback loop you can run, inspect, and improve.
