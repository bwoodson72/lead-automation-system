reframe: |-
  Freelancing can become an exhausting series of local fixes. Rewrite the website because leads are weak. Change the rate because a project was underpriced. Try outreach because referrals slowed down. Build a proposal template because the last deal got messy. Each fix can be sensible and still leave the business feeling fragile.

  The reason is dependency. Pricing is harder when the offer is vague. Outreach is harder when the buyer is broad. Sales calls drift when the problem is not defined. Delivery gets painful when scope and onboarding are improvised. Referrals and retainers remain accidental when the project simply ends. A weakness at one stage keeps leaking into the next.

  The Freelance Developer Launch Kit connects those stages in dependency order. You build enough clarity in market, offer, pricing, positioning, proof, acquisition, sales, onboarding, delivery, referrals, and recurring value for the next stage to stand on something solid. The goal is not a perfect business before you sell. It is a business with a shape, one you can inspect and improve instead of rebuilding from memory whenever something goes wrong.
