question: Which bundle should I choose?
answer: Choose the Freelancer Starter Bundle when offer, pricing, and marketing are all part of the same foundation problem. Choose the Client Acquisition Bundle when qualified opportunities are the main constraint and you want marketing, direct acquisition, social media, and agency partnerships working as one acquisition system. Choose the Freelancer Sales System when serious opportunities are reaching you but revenue is getting lost between sales conversations, proposals, closing, and post-project retention. Choose the Freelance Developer Business System when you want the Launch Kit plus the specialist systems so you can build the whole freelance business and go deeper as new bottlenecks appear. If only one area is weak, buying the focused product is the more disciplined choice.
