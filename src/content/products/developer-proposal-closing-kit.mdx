  A proposal is useful only after the important commercial questions are sufficiently understood. If scope is still vague, technical uncertainty is hidden, the buyer has not seen the price context, stakeholders are missing, or nobody knows how the decision will be made, a polished PDF does not solve the problem. It packages the ambiguity. That is why proposals get stuck in inboxes, revisions quietly expand the project, price objections arrive late, and a verbal yes can still turn into weeks of waiting for a signature or deposit. The Developer Proposal & Closing Kit starts with a readiness gate. You decide whether the opportunity deserves a proposal at all, translate discovery into an outcome and bounded scope, make assumptions and unknowns explicit, carry a defined price and payment policy into the document, and present the proposal around a real decision event. If the buyer pushes back, you diagnose what changed before conceding. If the project changes, you version the proposal or reopen discovery. Closed Won means the agreement and required payment are complete, not that the prospect sounded enthusiastic on a call.
