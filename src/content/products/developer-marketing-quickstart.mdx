faqs:
  - question: What exactly will I have when I finish?
    answer: You will have a defined business buyer, problem evidence, a bounded first offer and commercial route, buyer-facing message, account-addressable Primary channel, qualified First 25, execution record, diagnostic decision, and one-page record of the acquisition loop you can continue testing.
  - question: Is the workbook included?
    answer: Yes. The current edition includes a 45-page companion workbook with eight chapter-matched implementation records, completed Maya examples, a full First 25 prospect record, an execution record, a diagnostic review, and a final one-page acquisition-loop record.
  - question: Is this just a cold-email guide?
    answer: No. Direct outreach is the main execution path because it gives you identifiable businesses, a finite first cycle, and evidence you can inspect. The purpose is not to teach higher sending volume. It is to help you decide who deserves contact, why you have a legitimate reason to contact them, what you are testing, and what to do with the response.
  - question: Does the seven-day plan guarantee a client?
    answer: No. Seven days is an activation sequence, not a customer or revenue deadline. Follow-up may still be underway and more evidence may be needed. The first-week goal is to make the decisions, build the records, start the cycle, and know what happens next.
  - question: Does this teach pricing or do I need the pricing system first?
    answer: The Quickstart teaches enough pricing to keep the first offer from being economically blind. You estimate the delivery burden, work through the minimum economics the engagement needs to support, identify uncertainty, and decide whether you are ready to quote or whether another commercial step makes more sense. The Web Developer Pricing System goes much deeper.
  - question: Do I need another Developer Business Lab product first?
    answer: No. The Quickstart is designed to be a complete first acquisition exercise on its own. If you need to build the entire freelance business rather than solve the first acquisition problem, the Freelance Developer Launch Kit is the broader starting point.
