When your website says React, APIs, integrations, performance, automation, and custom development, the buyer still has to decide which capability matters, what problem you should solve, what the engagement includes, and why your price makes sense. That uncertainty is why every inquiry turns into a new scoping exercise and every proposal starts from a blank page. Productizing does not mean forcing every client into the same cookie-cutter package or pretending technical uncertainty does not exist. It means deciding in advance which buyer you are trying to help, which recurring problem you are prepared to solve, what outcome and boundaries define the engagement, and which parts of delivery should repeat.
