question: Should I buy the Freelance Developer Launch Kit or a focused product?
answer: Choose the Freelance Developer Launch Kit when several parts of the business are still unclear and you need the first complete operating system across offer, pricing, proof, acquisition, sales, delivery, cash, capacity, and future demand. Choose a focused product when you already know the specific constraint and want substantially deeper treatment of that one subsystem. The Launch Kit is not a prerequisite for the specialist products, and the specialist products are not chapters you must buy one by one before the Launch Kit makes sense.
