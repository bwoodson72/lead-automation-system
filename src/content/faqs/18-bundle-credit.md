question: Can I get credit for products I already own when buying a bundle?
answer: There is no automatic credit or upgrade pricing for products you already own. If you buy a bundle that includes a product you purchased individually and that earlier purchase is still within its 14-day refund period, buy the bundle first and then contact Developer Business Lab Support. Once the bundle purchase is verified, we can refund the overlapping individual purchase.
