question: Do I have to buy the products in a specific order?
answer: No. The catalog follows a business progression, but you should not buy products just to complete a sequence. Start with the constraint that is limiting the business now. Offer and pricing work often make later marketing and sales easier, but a developer with a working offer and a weak close does not need to go back and buy foundation products first. The bundles exist for cases where several connected problems are active at the same time.
