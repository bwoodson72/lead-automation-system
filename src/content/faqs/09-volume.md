question: Why focused systems instead of one giant freelancer course?
answer: Because the problem hurting the business now should not be buried behind ten unrelated modules. A developer with a pricing problem should be able to fix pricing without first completing a full acquisition curriculum. Focused products let you buy the smallest system that solves the current bottleneck, use it, and move on when another constraint actually matters.
