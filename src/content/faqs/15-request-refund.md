question: How do I request a refund?
answer: Submit the request through the Developer Business Lab Support Center within 14 days of purchase and choose Refund request as the support category. Include the email address used for the purchase, your order number if available, the product or bundle purchased, and a brief explanation of why you are requesting a refund. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
